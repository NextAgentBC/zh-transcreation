# Contributing

Short version: more real examples, sharper checklist items, honest sources.

- **Add examples** to `examples/`: English source (or the bad Chinese), the rewrite, and one or two lines on what changed and why. Label the origin and date. Only use text you wrote or that is public and short enough to quote.
- **Propose checklist items**: open an issue with a bad/good pair and the pattern behind it. Items must be about Chinese writing habits, not taste.
- **Quotes stay short**: a phrase or one sentence, with author and work. Paraphrase anything longer. Do not paste passages from published translations.
- **Sources**: every claim from research or a guideline needs a link that resolves. Check with `curl -I` before you send the PR.
- **Compliance rules** are advice for writers, not legal advice; say which jurisdiction a new rule comes from.
- Keep `SKILL.md` short. Detail goes in `method.md`.
